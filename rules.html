1. 台灣麻將是什麼

台灣麻將是四人遊戲，每人手上 16 張牌，比廣東、日本麻將的 13 張多。玩家輪流摸牌、打牌，誰先把手牌湊成規定的組合就「胡牌」，然後依照牌型的「台數」向其他玩家收錢。台灣麻將的特色是有 8 張花牌、可以「一路吃」上家的牌，而且就算沒有任何台也能胡（俗稱屁胡）。

2. 牌組（144 張）

萬子：一萬～九萬，每種 4 張，共 36 張
筒子：一筒～九筒，每種 4 張，共 36 張
索子（條子）：一索～九索，每種 4 張，共 36 張
風牌：東、南、西、北，每種 4 張，共 16 張
三元牌：紅中、青發、白板，每種 4 張，共 12 張
花牌：春夏秋冬、梅蘭竹菊，各 1 張，共 8 張
花牌不能用來組牌，摸到就亮出來放在旁邊，再補一張。花牌可以加台（見「正花」）。

萬、筒、索合稱「數牌」，風牌與三元牌合稱「字牌」。

4. 開局：抓風、發牌、補花

抓風定座位

傳統上把東南西北四張風牌蓋起來洗勻，每人摸一張，摸到哪個風就坐哪個位置，摸到「東」的人是第一任莊家。

本站做法依進房順序（大廳 1～4 號）輪流翻牌。輪到你時有 15 秒，沒翻系統會幫你翻。四人都翻完才會發牌。

發牌

每人拿 16 張，莊家多拿 1 張共 17 張，由莊家先打第一張牌。牌牆最後 16 張保留為「王牌」，專門用來補花與槓後補牌。

常見做法實體麻將會先擲骰子決定從哪裡開門、每次抓 4 張，本站由伺服器直接洗牌發牌，結果等同。

補花

開局時手上的花牌要全部亮出來，從王牌補同樣張數；補到花再繼續補。遊戲中摸到花也一樣立刻亮出並補牌。

5. 輪流摸牌與打牌

順序是 東 → 南 → 西 → 北（逆時針）。輪到你時先摸一張，手上變 17 張，再打出一張不要的牌，回到 16 張。打出的牌放在桌子中央，稱為「海底」或「河」。

本站操作點一下選牌、再點一下打出。剛摸的牌會放在最右邊。

6. 吃、碰、槓

別人打出的牌，你可以「拿走」來組成面子，但要把那組牌亮在桌上（副露）。

  • 吃：只能吃上家（坐你左手邊、在你前一家）打的數牌，組成順子。例如上家打 ，你有 就可以吃。
  • 碰：任何一家打出的牌，你手上有兩張一樣的就可以碰，組成刻子。碰完由你打牌，中間的玩家會被跳過。
  • 明槓：手上有三張一樣，別人又打出第四張，可以槓。
  • 暗槓：自己摸齊四張一樣的牌，可以宣告暗槓。暗槓不算破壞門清。
  • 加槓：已經碰出的刻子，又自己摸到第四張，可以加上去變成槓。

每次槓完都要從王牌補一張，再打牌。

搶槓：有人加槓時，如果那張牌正好是你要胡的牌，你可以搶過來胡（搶槓胡）。

7. 搶牌的優先順序

同一張牌有好幾個人要時：胡 > 碰／槓 > 吃。

兩個人以上都要胡同一張牌時：

  • 一炮一響（截胡）：由打牌者的下家開始，順序最近的那位胡。
  • 一炮多響：所有要胡的人都算胡，放槍者分別賠給每一位。
本站做法預設一炮一響。可以吃碰槓胡時畫面會跳出按鈕，有幾秒鐘（預設 8 秒）可以決定，時間到視為「過」。

8. 胡牌：放槍與自摸

  • 放槍（食胡）：別人打出的牌讓你胡了，打那張牌的人「放槍」，由他一個人付錢。
  • 自摸：自己摸到胡牌的那張，其他三家都要付錢。

台灣麻將沒有最低台數，0 台也可以胡（屁胡），只收「底」。

9. 聽牌、宣告聽牌、過水

聽牌：只差一張就能胡的狀態。你等的那幾種牌叫「聽的牌」。

本站：聽牌提示畫面會顯示你聽哪些牌、每種在桌面上還沒出現的張數（只扣掉大家打出的牌與亮出的副露，不扣你自己手裡的牌）。輪到你打牌時，「打了會聽牌」的牌上方會有金色標記。
本站：宣告聽牌（+1 台）選到打了會聽的牌時，可以按「聽牌」宣告。宣告後手牌鎖定：不能再吃、碰、槓，每次摸到不能胡的牌系統會自動打出；摸到能胡的牌會停下來讓你決定是否自摸。最後胡牌時多算 1 台「聽牌」。不宣告也可以照常胡，只是沒有這 1 台。

過水：別人打出你可以胡的牌，你卻選擇不胡，在你自己下一次摸牌之前，就不能再胡別人打出的牌（自摸不受影響）。這是為了防止「等更大的牌才胡」。

10. 流局

牌牆摸完（只剩王牌）還沒有人胡，就是流局，這一局不算錢。

11. 莊家、連莊與圈風

  • 莊家：坐東位、先打牌的人。莊家輸贏都會多算台（見下一節）。
  • 連莊：莊家胡牌，或是流局，莊家繼續當莊，稱為連莊。
  • 過莊：其他人胡牌，莊家換給下一家（南家當莊，依此類推）。
  • 圈風：四個人都當過一次莊算一圈。第一圈是「東風圈」，第二圈是「南風圈」。
本站做法一場打東風圈、南風圈兩圈，南風圈第 4 局結束（且莊家沒有連莊）就整場結束，寫入排行榜。每人起始 1000 點。

12. 算錢：底與台

開打前先約定「底」與「台」的金額，例如「50／20」代表底 50、每台 20。每一筆付款：

付款 ＝ 底 ＋ 台數 × 每台金額

  • 放槍：放槍的人付一筆給胡牌者。
  • 自摸：其他三家各付一筆。
  • 莊家台：只要胡牌者或付錢的人是莊家，這一筆就多算 1 台；莊家每連莊一次再加 2 台（俗稱「連 N 拉 N」）。也就是莊家台 ＝ 1 ＋ 2 × 連莊次數。
例 1：底 50／台 20。西家放槍給南家，南家這副牌 3 台，莊家是東家（沒參與）。
西家付 50 ＋ 3 × 20 ＝ 110。
例 2：同樣 50／20。東家當莊、已連莊 1 次，自摸 3 台。莊家台 ＝ 1 ＋ 2 × 1 ＝ 3。
南、西、北各付 50 ＋ (3 ＋ 3) × 20 ＝ 170，東家共收 510。

14. 各地差異與本站未計的台

台灣麻將沒有全國統一的規則台數與細節常因地區、家庭而不同，開打前請先跟牌友約好。常見差異：
  • 花槓：集齊春夏秋冬或梅蘭竹菊一組，很多人算 2 台；本站目前不計。
  • 無花：整局沒摸到花加 1 台；本站不計。
  • 槓上炮、河底撈魚（胡最後一張打出的牌）：有些地方各算 1 台；本站不計。
  • 七搶一、配牌花胡、地聽：本站台數表中有，但目前不會自動判定。
  • 高台牌型：大三元、大四喜、天胡等，有些地方算得更高（例如大三元 16 台、天胡 64 台）。
  • 正花對應：有些英文資料把西家寫成秋菊、北家寫成冬竹；本站依牌面的「梅 1、蘭 2、竹 3、菊 4」，西家是秋竹、北家是冬菊。
  • 一炮多響、過水、宣告聽牌：各地有些玩、有些不玩。

1. What is Taiwanese mahjong

Taiwanese mahjong is a four-player game where everyone holds 16 tiles, more than the 13 used in Cantonese or Japanese mahjong. Players take turns drawing and discarding until someone completes a winning hand, then collect payment based on the hand's value in tai. It uses 8 flower tiles, lets you chow from the player before you, and allows a win with zero tai.

2. The tiles (144)

Characters (萬): 1–9, four of each, 36 tiles
Dots (筒): 1–9, four of each, 36 tiles
Bamboo (索): 1–9, four of each, 36 tiles
Winds: East, South, West, North, four of each, 16 tiles
Dragons: Red, Green, White, four of each, 12 tiles
Flowers: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Plum, Orchid, Bamboo, Chrysanthemum, one each, 8 tiles
Flowers never stay in your hand. Reveal them and draw a replacement. Some flowers earn tai (see "matching flower").

Characters, dots and bamboo are suits. Winds and dragons are honors.

4. Setup: seats, deal, flowers

Drawing for seats

Traditionally the four wind tiles are shuffled face down and each player takes one. The wind you draw is your seat. East is the first dealer.

On this sitePlayers flip tiles one at a time in the order they joined (seats 1–4 in the lobby). You have 15 seconds on your turn, then a tile is flipped for you. Tiles are dealt after all four have drawn.

The deal

Everyone gets 16 tiles; the dealer gets 17 and discards first. The last 16 tiles of the wall form the dead wall, used only for flower and kong replacements.

Common variationAt a real table, dice decide where the wall is broken and tiles are taken four at a time. Here the server shuffles and deals directly, which is equivalent.

Replacing flowers

At the start, reveal every flower in your hand and draw the same number from the dead wall, repeating if you draw another flower. During play, a drawn flower is revealed and replaced immediately.

5. Taking turns

Play goes East → South → West → North (counter-clockwise). On your turn, draw a tile (17 in hand), then discard one (back to 16). Discards go to the middle of the table.

On this siteTap a tile to select it, tap again to discard. The tile you just drew is shown on the far right.

6. Chow, pong, kong

You may take another player's discard to complete a set, but that set is then exposed on the table.

  • Chow: only from the player before you (on your left), only suit tiles, to make a run. If they discard and you hold , you can chow.
  • Pong: from anyone, if you hold two matching tiles. You then discard, and players in between are skipped.
  • Exposed kong: you hold three and someone discards the fourth.
  • Concealed kong: you draw all four yourself. It does not break a concealed hand.
  • Added kong: you draw the fourth tile of a pong you already exposed.

After any kong, draw a replacement tile from the dead wall, then discard.

Robbing a kong: when someone adds to a pong and that tile completes your hand, you may win on it.

7. Call priority

When several players want the same discard: win > pong / kong > chow.

When several players can win on it:

  • Single winner: the first player after the discarder, in turn order, wins.
  • Multiple winners: everyone who can win does, and the discarder pays each of them.
On this siteSingle winner by default. When you can call, buttons appear and you have a few seconds (8 by default) to decide; otherwise it counts as a pass.

8. Winning: discard or self-draw

  • Win on a discard: only the player who discarded the winning tile pays.
  • Self-draw: you draw the winning tile yourself, and all three other players pay.

There is no minimum: a hand worth zero tai can still win, and is paid the base amount only.

9. Ready, declaring ready, furiten

Ready (tenpai): your hand is one tile away from winning. The tiles that would complete it are your waits.

On this site: tenpai helperThe table shows your waits and how many of each are still unseen. Only tiles everyone can see are subtracted: discards and exposed sets, not your own hand. On your turn, tiles that would leave you ready are marked with a gold bar.
On this site: declare ready (+1 tai)When you select a tile that leaves you ready, you can press "Declare ready". Your hand is then locked: no chow, pong or kong, and every drawn tile that does not win is discarded for you. If a drawn tile wins, play pauses so you can self-draw. Winning afterwards adds 1 tai. You can still win without declaring, just without that tai.

Temporary furiten: if you pass on a discard you could have won on, you cannot win on any discard until your next draw (self-draw is still allowed). This stops players waiting for a bigger win.

10. Draw games

If the live wall runs out (only the dead wall is left) and nobody has won, the hand is a draw and no one pays.

11. Dealer, streaks and rounds

  • Dealer: the East seat, who discards first. Payments involving the dealer earn extra tai (next section).
  • Dealer streak: if the dealer wins, or the hand is a draw, the dealer deals again.
  • Passing the deal: if anyone else wins, the next player becomes dealer.
  • Rounds: once all four have dealt, a round ends. The first round is East, the second is South.
On this siteA match is the East and South rounds. It ends after South 4 (unless the dealer keeps the deal), and the result goes to the leaderboard. Everyone starts with 1000 points.

12. Payments: base and tai

Before playing, agree on a base and a per-tai stake, e.g. "50 / 20". Each payment is:

payment = base + tai × per-tai stake

  • Win on a discard: the discarder makes one payment.
  • Self-draw: each of the other three makes one payment.
  • Dealer tai: if the winner or the payer is the dealer, that payment gets 1 extra tai, plus 2 more for each streak. Dealer tai = 1 + 2 × streak count.
Example 1: 50 / 20. West discards and South wins with 3 tai; East is dealer and not involved.
West pays 50 + 3 × 20 = 110.
Example 2: 50 / 20. East is dealer on a 1-hand streak and self-draws with 3 tai. Dealer tai = 1 + 2 × 1 = 3.
South, West and North each pay 50 + (3 + 3) × 20 = 170; East collects 510.

14. Variations not scored here

There is no single official rulesetValues and details differ by region and household, so agree on them before playing. Common differences:
  • Flower kong: collecting all four seasons or all four plants, often 2 tai. Not scored here.
  • No flowers: 1 tai for having no flowers. Not scored here.
  • Win after a kong discard, win on the last discard: 1 tai each in some places. Not scored here.
  • Seven flowers robbing one, dealer-ready flower, earthly ready: in this site's table, but not detected automatically yet.
  • Big hands: some groups score them higher (e.g. big three dragons 16, heavenly win 64).
  • Matching flowers: some English sources give West Autumn + Chrysanthemum and North Winter + Bamboo. This site follows the numbers printed on Taiwanese tiles (Plum 1, Orchid 2, Bamboo 3, Chrysanthemum 4).
  • Multiple winners, furiten and declaring ready: played by some groups and not others.
